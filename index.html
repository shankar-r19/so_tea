Our story

Personal hygiene isn’t just about being clean. It’s about comfort, confidence, and feeling good in your own body.

so tea began with a simple ritual: the first cup of the morning. The quiet before the day starts. Warm hands, soft light, nowhere to be yet. That feeling of being safe, unhurried and entirely yourself.

We wanted to make clothes that hold that feeling all day. Underwear and loungewear closest to your skin, made from a luxurious fabric that stays fresh, breathes easily and feels like a deep exhale. Every piece is named after a tea and dyed in its colour, because the things we keep closest should feel like a small comfort.
